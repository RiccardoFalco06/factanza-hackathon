Questions

What you'll ask us

Why Zanichelli?

It brings the authority of a dictionary everyone in Italy knows from school, and Factanza doesn't have to write definitions from scratch. The partnership in the prototype is a proposal, not a done deal.

Aren't quizzes too school-like?

Five questions a week, in Factanza's voice, with a streak to keep alive. More game than homework, and every answer leads to the piece that explains it.

Why not ask people to sign up right away?

Every extra screen at the door loses people. You get in straight away, and the account is requested once there's something to save: progress, quizzes, followed stories.

How does it make money?

People who stay longer and come back often see the Academy, the subscription and events at the right moment. Spaces inside quizzes and stories can also host partners that fit the topic.
